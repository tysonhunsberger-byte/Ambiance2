The VANGELIS SYNTH - 128 patches for the ARMINATOR 2
by KRAKLI SYNTHS

A emulation of the legendary Yamaha CS80

A very good update of the ARMINATOR - here are the sounds that use these new features.


The Yamaha CS80 has this special sound and for sure the famoust user was Vangelis Papathanassiou.


In these bank I´ve tried to recreate all the wonderful sounds of the first "Blade Runner" movie,

that doesn´t fit in the SuperSetCollection - and further all those sounds, Mr Wallfisch and

Mr Hans Zimmer used in their soundtrack in the "Blade Runner 2049" movie. I think, I did on

some patches.


You can find all those atmospheric sounds, SoundFX of Blade Runner, like the LAPD Pods,

the soundatmos (POlice sirens down far far in the downstreets), Street noises etc.,

the lush strings, majestic Brasses and unusual sounds and typical CS-sounds.

Many sounds did use the new split-function....it´s another kind of playing.

Most Pads and Leads or Sequence/ARP-sounds with Sololeads or Pads....or a combination of

SFX-Sounds.....Try them all...

Most patches are soundtracks in themself - little islands on the ocean - so good for 

cinematic feel, progressive electronic and dreamy atmospheres....

In combination with the SuperCollection this is outstanding....

Please have listen to this sounddemo and you may expect what you get.....

All sounds are from the soundbank (except of the Drums/real Percussion) and played 

without the use of external effects....




You may find many "Leads, Pads, Basses, SFX, Ringmodulator-sounds, Strings, Keys, Plucked, 
Arpeggiator- and Sequencesounds, Dual- and Splitsounds in this bank....


Important: Use the Modwheel and "play" these patches - most patches depends how to play.....
                
Also play those "Vangelis-like" chords or fewer notes with the Arpeggiator-sounds.....
                


Enjoy!
